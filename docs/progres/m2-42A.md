  `WargaData`; the old fields stay only as the 42-off fallback (design: 42 off = 38 exactly). No mati/gen records are
  written while 42 is on (Penerus.mati).
- Empty slots (death, retirement, sect, move) are absent until 42A-2 Pekerjaan fills them.
- Enemy avoidance is a glare + angry face when passing (not a path-cost change); `kota_suasana` reaction not done.
