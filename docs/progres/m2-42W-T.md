`tools/gen_tempat_tema_{pelabuhan,oasis,air,gunung,iblis}.py`, regenerated `structure/kota_tema/**` (+ their pools /

not `gen_tempat_tema_{data,satelit,*_cerita}.py`.

## Layout decisions

- No room inside the 3x3 grids: every new lot is an OFF-GRID free lot (dx/dz not both multiples of 48, no side code)
  OUTSIDE the town wall, inside the district keep-out zone (+-120 = SETENGAH_DISTRIK 72 + MARGIN 48), so roads skip it
  (JalanStructure skips born zones) and foreign structures are refused (TempatKeepOut). Gate corridor (city x +-48)
  stays free.
- Per theme (not iblis): south band west of the gate `hidup42/jalan_malam` (city x -96..-49) + `hidup42/kavling_barat`
  (-144..-97); east of the gate `hidup42/kantor_rekrut` (48..95) + `hidup42/kavling_timur` (96..143); themed
  `hidup42/makam` outside the west wall of a middle/bottom-row district (dx -106..-112). 10 kavling (5 per lot),
  lapak_sewa 5 (2 night street + 3 kantor forecourt), pasar_hantu 4 (gang_buntu + 2 dekat_makam + gudang_kosong in kavling_timur).
- Iblis: no kavling gaya / no makam in 42.12.1 -> `hidup42/kantor_rekrut` (SE, near Zona Perjanjian kedai_racun) and
  `hidup42/lorong_hantu` (SW: pawnshop, talisman shop, lapak_sewa 5, pasar_hantu 4); jalan_malam = pasar iblis.
- Preview: scratchpad w42b/peta.py (iso/top of NBT); my scripts in scratchpad w42t/.
