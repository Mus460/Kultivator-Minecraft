## Butuh dari modul lain
- 42D (barongsai): the lion mesh draws its two dancers' legs itself (4 trousered legs, fur cuffs, clawed boots) and
  animates them per movement, so spawning dancer bodies on clips 378/379 inside it would double the legs: either do
  not spawn them, or D-42V hides `kaki_*` when bodies are present. The renderer reads `nilai()` = jaw 0..1, `kedip()`,
  `keadaan()`/`mulai()`; one-shot lengths: MENYAPA 60, BANGUN 41, MELOMPAT 40, MAKAN_SELADA 92 (spit at 60-92),
  MENGGELENG 45 ticks; JALAN steps every LANGKAH_TICK.
- 42D-H (animals): one-shot states read from `mulai()` and should last at least: cat MEREGANG 50, LOMPAT 26; chicken
  KOKOK 40 (PATUK repeats every 16); horse/donkey HENTAK 40, ANGGUK 50, donkey MERINGKIK 60, DENGUS 40; camel
  BERLUTUT folds in 40 (stand-up is instant when it leaves BERLUTUT). Donkey pack bags show while `nilai() > 0.5`.
  Pigeons: set `benih` (varied per flock) and `jumlah` (8-16) at spawn; `nilai()` = circling radius (0 -> 4 blocks);
  the flock sim is relative to the entity, so move the entity only slowly. Entity names (lang) are 42D-H's.
- D-42V: optional polish: a camel get-up animation needs a synced "bangun" state (not among 42R0's constants).
