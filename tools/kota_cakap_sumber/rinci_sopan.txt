# rinci_sopan: D-42K detail pass. The register "sopan" is what traders, careful people, officials and anyone facing a stranger
# speak (Topik.register), yet the corpus had almost no sopan frames: they fell back to the plain base text. These frames are
# tagged [sopan] (only said in that register); every rule shares niat/topik/syarat with an old one, so the planner pools them.
@kelompok rinci
