# rinci: D-42K detail pass (conversation corpus breadth, DESIGN 42.5A.8). Source format: gen_kota_hidup_cakap.py.
# Glue fragments that decorate the base frames of BERI_KABAR, KELUH, SAPA and BASA_BASI (an opener ends with a sentence stop
# and a glued space "\_", a tail starts with a glued space), so one frame yields many sentences. The slots are added to the
# frames by the drafting aid (scratchpad d42k/decor.py); the rules below are the fragments themselves.
@kelompok rinci
