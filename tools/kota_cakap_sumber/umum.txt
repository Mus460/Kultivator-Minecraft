== basa.cuaca.langit
niat: BASA_BASI
topik: cuaca
syarat: cuaca=gerhana,meteor
gestur: tatap_langit
- [3] Langit aneh sekali hari ini. Ibuku sudah membakar dupa tiga batang. || The sky is so strange today. My mother has burned three incense sticks.
  ~ kasar: Langit gila. Jangan tanya aku kenapa. || Mad sky. Don't ask me why.
  ~ kuno: Langit menunjukkan pertanda. Para tetua sekte pasti sedang menghitung bintang. || Heaven shows an omen. The sect elders must be counting the stars.
  ~ kampung: Langit kayak gitu, Nak, kata simbah tanda ada raja baru lahir. || A sky like that, child, granny says, means a new king is born.
  ~ pelabuhan: Langit begini, Bung, para nakhoda tidak mau berlayar. Takut dewa laut marah. || A sky like this, mate, the captains won't sail. Afraid the sea god's angry.
- [2] Kau lihat bintang jatuh semalam? Aku sempat membuat tiga permohonan! || Did you see the falling stars last night? I made three wishes!
  ~ p: Bintang jatuh semalam! Aku memohon jodoh yang baik untuk anakku. || Falling stars last night! I wished for a good match for my child.
- Anak-anak takut sekali waktu matahari menghilang. Aku juga, sebenarnya. || The children were terrified when the sun vanished. So was I, honestly.
  ~ gunung: Para biksu memukul lonceng terus waktu matahari tertutup. Amitaba. || The monks rang the bell nonstop while the sun was covered. Amitabha.
